06 / 11 · Independent watchmaker

Aurum

A watch configurator where the hands show your real time and the whole watch comes apart as you scroll.

Open the live site ↗View the code ↗

The signature moment

The exploded view

Through an open-heart window the balance wheel swings in real time. Choose the case, dial and strap and the watch changes live; keep scrolling and it separates layer by layer — crystal, hands, dial, case, movement, caseback — under cool steel light.

Try this

Change the dial, then scroll to take the watch apart.

How it's built

  • Case, bezel and crystal are lathe-turned; the lugs are extruded shapes
  • The dial is painted on a canvas at runtime, with today's date in the window
  • A scroll-linked exploded view of six layers

Built with

  • Next.js 16
  • React Three Fiber
  • Three.js
  • GLSL shaders
  • TypeScript

Product brands, jewellery, luxury goods and online shops.
